FAQ

Straight answers

Does Nidhi Farms grow these crops?

No. The name is the brand. We buy herbs and other ethical products from sellers and supply bulk buyers. We do not raise the plants.

What vetiver forms can you supply?

Dried khus roots, vetiver oil, khus mats, curtains and fans, and vetiver grass plants or slips. Tell us which one you need. They are not interchangeable.

Which other herbs are on the list?

Ashwagandha root, shatavari root, moringa leaf and powder, brahmi (Bacopa monnieri) and tulsi leaf. Also kapoor kachri, turmeric, khapli wheat, neem leaf, millets, isabgol, jaggery and selected oils.

Do you make disease or treatment claims?

No. We supply goods to businesses. We do not claim that any product treats, cures or prevents a disease.

Can I get a sample and a lab report?

Yes. Both are available on request for the lot being quoted.

What is the minimum order?

It varies by product and by the seller’s lot. Send the quantity you need. We confirm availability in the USD quote.

Where are you based?
