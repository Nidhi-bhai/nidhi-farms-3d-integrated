FAQ

Straight answers

Is Nidhi Farms a farm or a grower?

No. The name is the brand. The business is trading: we buy herbs and other ethical products from vetted sellers and supply bulk buyers. We do not cultivate, and we do not sell farm-direct from our own land.

What can you supply?

Herbs and other ethical products. Current examples are ashwagandha, shatavari, kapoor kachri, and vetiver roots and plants, plus moringa, turmeric, khapli wheat, millets, neem leaf, isabgol, jaggery and selected oils. If you need a different ethical product, ask.

Are the products organically certified?

We do not claim organic certification. If a seller holds a certificate for a specific lot, we can pass that document on with the quote. It applies only to that lot.

Do you make health or medical claims?

No. We supply ingredients and other goods to businesses. We do not claim that any product treats, cures or prevents a disease.

Can I get a sample and a lab report?

Yes. Both are available on request for the lot being quoted. Ask for them in the message on the enquiry form.

What is the minimum order?

It varies by product and by the seller’s lot. Send the quantity you need. We confirm availability and any minimum in the USD quote.

Which currency do you quote in?

US dollars.

Where are you based?

Tanuku, West Godavari, Andhra Pradesh, India. Dispatch is arranged from there. GSTIN 37ARKPK0686M1Z6.
